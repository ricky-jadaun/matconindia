About Matcon India

Building Business Through Relationships & Reliability

Matcon India is a B2B construction-material supply and liaison organization specializing primarily in institutional and non-trade cement.

We work with builders, contractors, RMC plants, infrastructure companies and institutional buyers, coordinating their requirements with leading cement manufacturers.

Our role extends beyond simply quoting a cement price.

We support the customer through the complete commercial journey—from identifying the suitable brand and grade to manufacturer coordination, customer-code opening, technical approval where required, order and payment coordination, dispatch scheduling and supply follow-up.

Our experience across multiple manufacturers and markets allows us to understand that every project is different. Brand approvals, customer-code status, freight economics, project location, pricing and availability can all affect the right procurement decision.

Matcon therefore focuses on providing customers with responsive, practical and dependable coordination rather than a one-size-fits-all solution.
