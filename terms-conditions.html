2. Nature of Organization & Commercial Role

Matcon India is a specialized B2B construction-material supply and liaison organization operating in the institutional and non-trade cement segment. Our services encompass coordinating procurement, pricing, allocation, and direct plant-to-site supply between institutional buyers (builders, developers, contractors, and RMC plant operators) and authorized national cement manufacturers (including UltraTech, Ambuja, ACC, JK Lakshmi, Shree Cement, Dalmia, and other leading producers).

Depending on the specific supply arrangement and manufacturer channel structure, billing may be issued directly by the manufacturing company under an institutional customer code or through an authorized channel partner, as agreed prior to order confirmation.

3. Quotations, Pricing & Market Volatility

Due to the dynamic nature of the institutional cement market, all price indications and quotations are subject to strict commercial parameters:

  • Dynamic Non-Trade Pricing: Non-trade cement prices depend on prevailing plant base prices, freight indices, diesel costs, unloading location pin codes, applicable GST rates, and manufacturer-mandated monthly or daily pricing revisions.
  • Validity Window: Unless explicitly stated in writing, quotations provided by Matcon India (verbally, via WhatsApp, email, or formal quotation sheet) are indicative and time-bound, valid strictly for 24 to 48 hours or until the same-day price closing mandated by the supplying cement plant.
  • Order Confirmation: Quotations do not constitute a reserved allocation until: (a) verification of active customer code, (b) receipt of formal Purchase Order (PO), (c) complete realization of advance payment, and (d) formal acceptance of the loading indent by the supplying manufacturer plant.
  • Statutory Variations: Any changes in statutory duties, GST rates, central/state transit levies, or manufacturer-notified freight revisions occurring prior to actual plant loading shall be payable by the Buyer.

4. Institutional Customer Code Opening & Documentation

Procuring non-trade cement directly from primary manufacturers requires the establishment and active approval of an institutional customer code:

  • Authentic Documentation: The Buyer agrees to furnish authentic, verifiable, and current documentation, including GST registration certificate, PAN card, company registration documents, project sanction letters, RERA registration (where applicable), work orders, and site geo-coordinates.
  • Project-Specific Use: The Buyer confirms that the customer code opened through Matcon India’s coordination is strictly tied to the designated project site and shall be utilized solely for bonafide project consumption.
  • Manufacturer Approval: Code generation and commercial terms are subject to the independent credit, technical, and commercial approvals of the respective cement manufacturer. Matcon India facilitates and coordinates the process but does not guarantee manufacturer underwriting decisions.

5. Payment Terms & Financial Protocol

All institutional supply operations adhere strictly to established industry financial disciplines:

  • 100% Advance Payment: In accordance with standard institutional cement procurement norms, all supplies are executed strictly against 100% advance payment via RTGS, NEFT, or direct verified banking transfers, unless the Buyer holds an active, manufacturer-approved direct credit facility.
  • Fund Realization & Loading Indent: Factory vehicle loading indents are queued only upon confirmed realization of funds in the designated manufacturer or billing entity bank account. Matcon India assumes no responsibility for plant dispatch delays caused by banking network delays, uncredited transfers, or missing UTR details.
  • Financial Charges: Any bank charges, RTGS fees, or payment gateway deductions shall be borne solely by the Buyer. Invoicing is generated against net credited amounts.

6. Plant-to-Site Dispatch, Logistics & Site Responsibilities

Non-trade cement is dispatched directly from manufacturer plants or regional grinding terminals via dedicated heavy commercial vehicles (multi-axle trucks, trailers, or bulkers). The Buyer agrees to the following site and logistics covenants:

  • Site Access & Approvals: The Buyer is solely responsible for ensuring clear, motorable, heavy-vehicle-accessible roads up to the unloading point at the project site. The Buyer must arrange all local permissions, municipal clearances, and “No Entry” window passes required for heavy truck entry into municipal zones.
  • Unloading Protocol: Unloading is the sole operational and financial responsibility of the Buyer, unless explicitly agreed otherwise in a written contract.
  • Vehicle Turnaround & Demurrage: Delivery vehicles must be unloaded and released within the standard industry turnaround window (strictly within 3 to 6 hours of vehicle arrival at the designated site). Any vehicle detention charges, waiting fees, or transporter demurrage arising from site unloading delays, labor unavailability, local disputes, or inaccessible site entry shall be payable directly by the Buyer.
  • Inspection & Transit Claims: The Buyer’s authorized site representative must inspect bag counts, packaging integrity, and manufacturer batch stamps upon vehicle arrival before unloading commences. In the rare event of transit damage, burst bags, or quantity shortages, an endorsement must be recorded on the Consignee Copy of the Lorry Receipt (LR) / e-Way bill and counter-signed by the vehicle driver. Failure to endorse the physical LR upon arrival constitutes complete and irrevocable acceptance of the consignment in sound condition.

7. Strict Prohibition on Retail Trade Diversion & Resale

Non-trade institutional cement is supplied at institutional rates exclusively for direct consumption on the project registered under the customer code:

  • Zero-Tolerance on Diversion: Re-selling, diverting, transferring, or retailing institutional cement to retail dealers, retail shops, or unauthorized third-party sites is strictly prohibited by law, GST regulations, and manufacturer agreements.
  • Legal & Commercial Penalties: Any violation of this non-diversion covenant shall result in immediate customer code blacklisting, cancellation of pending dispatches, penalty assessments by the manufacturer, and legal proceedings under applicable commercial and tax statutes. The Buyer agrees to indemnify Matcon India against all claims arising from unauthorized material diversion.

8. Quality Standards, Manufacturer Test Certificates (MTC) & Warranty

All cement coordinated by Matcon India is sourced directly from BIS-certified manufacturing facilities of leading cement companies:

  • BIS Compliance: Cement conforms to Bureau of Indian Standards specifications, including IS 1489 (Portland Pozzolana Cement - PPC), IS 8112 (Ordinary Portland Cement - OPC 43 Grade), and IS 12269 (Ordinary Portland Cement - OPC 53 Grade).
  • Manufacturer Test Certificate (MTC): Every dispatched batch is accompanied by the official MTC issued by the manufacturing plant’s quality control laboratory verifying physical and chemical test properties.
  • Warranty Disclaimer: As Matcon India acts as a liaison and supply coordination organization and is not the original manufacturer of cement, all product warranties, compressive strength guarantees, and quality commitments are provided directly and exclusively by the producing manufacturer. Matcon India disclaims all independent, express, or implied warranties of merchantability or fitness for a particular structural purpose.

9. Cancellation, Modification & Re-Routing Policy

Due to automated plant loading queue allocations, digital GST e-way bill generation, and dedicated freight vehicle dispatch:

  • Post-Loading Irrevocability: Once a vehicle has been loaded at the cement plant and the e-way bill has been generated, orders cannot be cancelled, modified, or held in transit.
  • Re-Routing Expenses: Any site change or vehicle diversion requested after dispatch is subject to formal manufacturer approval, cancellation of existing e-way bills, re-issuance fees, and additional freight charges payable in advance by the Buyer.
