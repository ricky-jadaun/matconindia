How Matcon Works

From Requirement to Dispatch

A transparent, structured 7-step process ensuring verified brand options, direct manufacturer supply, and on-time site dispatch.

01

Share Your Requirement

Tell us the project location, cement grade, approximate quantity and preferred brand, if any.

02

Brand & Price Coordination

We coordinate suitable manufacturer options and prevailing institutional pricing.

03

Approval & Customer Code

Where required, Matcon coordinates documents, samples/testing, brand approval and customer-code opening.

04

Order & Payment

After commercial confirmation, PO/payment requirements are coordinated with the relevant manufacturer.

05

Dispatch Schedule

Customer shares the required delivery schedule according to site consumption.

06

Factory Dispatch

The cement manufacturer dispatches material against the approved order and schedule.

07

Supply Follow-Up

Matcon coordinates dispatch status and subsequent requirements for smooth repeat business.
