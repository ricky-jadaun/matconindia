Last Updated: September 2026 | Matcon India B2B Privacy Notice

1. Introduction & Corporate Scope

Matcon India (“Matcon”, “we”, “our”, or “us”) is a dedicated B2B construction-material supply and liaison organization coordinating institutional and non-trade cement procurement for builders, infrastructure contractors, real estate developers, and RMC batching plants across India. We coordinate directly with leading national cement manufacturers to facilitate plant-to-site supply, customer code creation, and wholesale commercial arrangements.

This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, process, manage, and safeguard commercial, personal, and project-related data gathered through our website (matconindia.in), digital communication channels, WhatsApp business inquiries, and procurement liaison operations in compliance with applicable Indian data protection laws, including the Information Technology Act, 2000, the IT (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

2. Information We Collect

In our role as an institutional cement supply and manufacturer liaison partner, we collect information necessary to prepare quotations, evaluate project eligibility, open institutional customer codes, and coordinate direct factory dispatches. We categorize collected information as follows:

  • Business & Contact Data: Registered entity name, trade name, Goods and Services Tax Identification Number (GSTIN), Permanent Account Number (PAN), corporate identity proof, authorized contact person’s name, designation, official email address, mobile/telephone numbers, and registered office address.
  • Project & Site Delivery Data: Construction site physical address, delivery pin code, project type (residential, commercial, infrastructure, roadworks, or RMC), estimated project duration, site access conditions for heavy commercial vehicles, and site receiving supervisor contact details.
  • Institutional Customer Code KYC Documents: Where client authorization is given to open or coordinate non-trade customer accounts directly with cement manufacturing companies (such as UltraTech, Ambuja, ACC, JK Lakshmi, Shree Cement, Dalmia, etc.), we collect required manufacturer documentation including GST registration certificates, PAN cards, cancelled company cheques, project sanction letters/RERA certificates, and authorized signatory specimen signatures.
  • Procurement & Commercial Records: Cement grade preferences (PPC, OPC 43, OPC 53), estimated monthly and aggregate order volumes, brand approvals, daily quotation records, purchase orders (POs), RTGS/NEFT payment transaction reference numbers, and dispatch records. Note: We do not store net-banking passwords, credit/debit card numbers, or UPI PINs on our servers.
  • Technical & Analytical Information: Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, device type, geographic location data, operating system, pages visited on our website, and interaction timestamps collected automatically via standard cookies and log files.

3. How We Collect Information

We collect information through multiple commercial touchpoints:

  • Direct Submissions: When you complete website enquiry forms, request daily price quotes, contact our team via phone or email, or communicate through our official WhatsApp business channel (+91 70421 78282).
  • Liaison & Onboarding: When you provide company and project documents to facilitate institutional customer code creation with cement manufacturers.
  • Transaction Execution: During the coordination of purchase orders, advance payment confirmations, plant loading queues, and delivery acknowledgments.
  • Automated Technologies: Through web browser cookies, server logs, and analytics tools as you navigate through our digital portal.

4. Purpose of Data Processing

We process collected data exclusively for legitimate commercial, operational, and statutory purposes:

  • Price Quotations & Feasibility: Evaluating project requirements and calculating accurate, location-specific non-trade cement price quotes based on real-time manufacturer plant prices and transport tariffs.
  • Manufacturer Code Processing: Submitting verified documentation to primary cement manufacturers on your behalf for institutional customer code registration and credit/order approvals.
  • Dispatch & Logistics Management: Coordinating with manufacturer logistics desks and freight carriers to schedule plant loading, vehicle movement, e-way bill generation, and direct delivery to designated project sites.
  • Commercial Communications: Sharing official proforma invoices, dispatch notices, Manufacturer Test Certificates (MTC), payment receipts, and delivery confirmations.
  • Statutory & Regulatory Compliance: Maintaining commercial records to comply with GST regulations, corporate taxation requirements, and audit mandates under Indian law.

5. Sharing and Disclosure of Information

Matcon India maintains strict business confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or business data to external marketing agencies. Data is disclosed strictly under the following operational circumstances:

  • Cement Manufacturing Companies: KYC documents, GST certificates, and project site addresses are shared with approved cement manufacturers (e.g., UltraTech, Ambuja, ACC, JK Lakshmi, Shree, Dalmia) strictly for opening your institutional customer code and issuing direct factory invoices.
  • Logistics & Transport Carriers: Authorized truck drivers, fleet operators, and freight coordinators receive delivery destination addresses and site contact numbers strictly to facilitate vehicle entry, transit tracking, and safe unloading.
  • Statutory Authorities: Disclosed when mandated by applicable law, GST departmental inquiries, court orders, or government investigative authorities.
  • Professional Advisors: Chartered accountants, auditors, and legal counsels bound by professional confidentiality obligations.

6. Data Security & Confidentiality

We deploy rigorous technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your business documents and personal data against unauthorized access, accidental destruction, alteration, or disclosure. Internal access to client KYC documents, banking confirmations, and site details is restricted strictly to authorized Matcon India procurement managers and logistics coordinators bound by non-disclosure protocols.

7. Data Retention

We retain your business and personal data only for as long as necessary to satisfy the commercial purposes for which it was obtained, including the duration of active project supply cycles, ongoing manufacturer code maintenance, warranty support, and statutory record-keeping periods mandated by Indian tax and commercial statutes (typically up to 8 financial years under GST and Income Tax laws).

8. Your Rights & Preferences

Under applicable Indian data protection principles, clients and website visitors possess clear rights regarding their data:

  • Access & Review: You may request a summary of the business or contact information held by Matcon India regarding your account.
  • Correction & Updating: You may request prompt correction of inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete company details, contact numbers, or project addresses.
  • Withdrawal of Consent: You may withdraw consent for commercial updates, price broadcasts, or marketing alerts at any time by notifying us in writing.
  • Erasure: You may request the deletion of personal information where continued retention is no longer necessary for ongoing supply contracts, active customer codes, or statutory compliance.

9. Cookies and Web Analytics

Our website utilizes standard session and analytical cookies to remember user preferences, measure website traffic, and optimize site responsiveness. Cookies do not extract confidential files from your device. You can configure your browser to decline cookies, though certain interactive features of our portal may have limited functionality as a result.

10. Third-Party Websites & Brand Links

Our website contains references and links to third-party manufacturer portals and social media networks (e.g., LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube). Matcon India does not exercise control over third-party platforms. When navigating external websites, please review their respective privacy policies, as Matcon India assumes no liability for their data practices.

11. Policy Modifications

Matcon India reserves the right to amend or update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in regulatory standards, industry practices, or operational procedures. Revised versions will be published on this page with an updated “Last Updated” date. Continued engagement with our services constitutes acceptance of the modified policy.
